Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Contempla ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, and optional handle when you create an account.
  • Practice data: meditation session history (duration, technique, date) that you log in the app.
  • Content you create: meditation techniques you add to your personal library or submit to the global library.
  • Usage data: basic analytics on how features are used, to improve the app.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app and its features.
  • To enable community features such as the activity feed and friend connections, if you opt in.
  • To process subscription payments via Apple's in-app purchase system.
  • To send optional practice reminders you configure in the app.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database and authentication infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Apple — in-app purchases and Sign in with Apple.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Practice data and account information are not shared with third parties except as necessary to operate the services listed above.

Community Features

If you use community features (activity feed, friends), your display name and session activity may be visible to users you connect with. You can control this in Settings → Privacy.

Data Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Support → Delete Account, or by contacting us.
